## Questions businesses ask

### Do we need Enterprise?

Start with what your application needs. Enterprise is an option for specific requirements, not a prerequisite for using Shpyrd as a business.

### Can we use our company login?

We can confirm SSO support for your identity provider and the offer that fits your setup.

### Do we have to move everything?

Start with one application. Use that experience to decide whether Shpyrd fits more of your applications.

### What support do we get?

The selected offer defines the support channels, coverage, and any response commitments. We can match those to your application's needs.
