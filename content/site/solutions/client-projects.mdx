## Questions partners ask

### What does a handover include?

Agree on the handover, including access, accounts, contracts, credentials, documentation, and operating responsibilities.

### Do I have to resell the infrastructure?

No. Your client can contract directly with Shpyrd, while you hand over or keep maintaining the app.

### Can the project run in my client's cloud?

Shpyrd can run on AWS and Oracle Cloud. The selected offer defines who operates that environment.
