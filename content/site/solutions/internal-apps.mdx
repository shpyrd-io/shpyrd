<Questions>

## Practical questions

### Does it matter how the app was built?

No. Built with AI or conventionally, Shpyrd deploys it and controls who can open it.

### Does Shpyrd set up the approval rules in my app?

No. Your app defines its process and its business permissions; Shpyrd controls access to the app.

### Can we use our company login?

We can confirm SSO support for your identity provider and the offer that fits your setup.

</Questions>
