<Questions>

## Questions people ask

### Can my site have a form?

Yes, when the form is built into your site's code or a service it connects to.

### Is there a site builder or CMS?

No. You build the site, with your agent or your tools; Shpyrd puts it online and keeps it updated.

### My site needs a database.

Then it is an app: see Web Apps & APIs for apps with databases and workers.
