## Practical questions

### Does Shpyrd invoice my clients?

No. You set your prices and handle your clients' billing; Shpyrd bills you.

### Who supports the applications?

Shpyrd operates the infrastructure. Application work stays with you, as agreed with each client.

### Is this the same as running in my client's cloud?

No. Self-hosting in a client's cloud and Shpyrd-managed infrastructure have different responsibilities.
