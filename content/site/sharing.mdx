# Intro

## Title

Getting Started

## Lead

From your idea to a live app or website in three steps.

# Steps

<Record>

## Id

build

## Title

Build with your favorite AI

## Body

Create your app or website with the AI you already love. Make it yours, try it out, and get it ready to share.

## Note

### Title

Start with one useful thing.

### Body

A page for your business, a tool for your team, or a side project. Get the first version working, then ask your AI to improve it as you go.

</Record>

<Record>

## Id

publish

## Title

Ask shpyrd to put it online

## Body

Tell your AI to deploy with shpyrd. shpyrd takes care of getting your app or website onto the internet and gives you a link to open it.

## Note

### Title

Connect shpyrd to your AI first.

### Body

Add shpyrd to your AI tool and sign in. Then ask it to deploy the project you’ve been working on, right from the same conversation.

</Record>

<Record>

## Id

share

## Title

Give access. Bring everyone in.

## Body

Your app or website is live at its own address. Make it public for everyone, or keep it private and give access to the people or teams you choose.

## Note

### Title

All their apps, in one place.

### Body

The people you give access to can sign in to the launcher, see all the apps available to them, and open any of them with a click. No hunting through messages for the right link.

</Record>
