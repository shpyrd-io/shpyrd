# Route

## Heading

From your laptop to your team

## Description

The same agent that built the app takes it the rest of the way.

## Steps

| Id | Title | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| connect | Connect your agent to your workspace. | One line in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. |
| deploy | Deploy the app. | It gets an address, TLS, and a sign-in in front of it. Nobody gets in yet. |
| share | Share it with the people it is for. | A team, or named colleagues. They find it among their apps the next time they sign in. |
| change | Keep changing it. | Each update is a release your colleagues get without asking; a bad one is undone in one step. |
