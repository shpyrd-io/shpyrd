<Questions>

## Practical questions

### Does rolling back restore my data?

No. Rollback returns application code and configuration. Database records and files are recovered from backups.

### Can my agent help me find a problem?

Yes. Through MCP it can inspect your project's status, logs, and metrics, and explain what it sees.

### Does it scale with traffic on its own?

Capacity is adjustable: you choose the process counts and resource sizes.

</Questions>
