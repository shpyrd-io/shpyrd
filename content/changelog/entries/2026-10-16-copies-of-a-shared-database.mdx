---
title: "Nightly copies of a shared database"
description: "A copy every night, 7, 14 or 30 kept, one on demand, and any of them restored as a new database."
date: "2026-10-16"
type: feature
draft: false
---

A shared database gets a copy every night, and the newest 7, 14 or 30 stay (`shpyrd pg backups enable --retention 14d`). `shpyrd pg backup` takes one now. `shpyrd pg restore <database> --as <new>` brings the newest copy, or the one you name, back as a new database, so the one you have keeps running while you look.
