title: "Your account lists every workspace you can open"
description: "Products in shpyrd Accounts shows the workspaces you were invited to, not only those you created."
date: "2026-10-23"
type: improvement
draft: false
---

**Products** in shpyrd Accounts listed only the workspaces you created. It now lists every workspace you are a member of, with your role, and opens them from there. Signing in no longer sends someone who was invited to a workspace to the page for creating one: anyone with a workspace goes to Products.
