A project's web process and a dedicated database can be on one of three settings: a quiet period of their own, never, or the workspace's default. Until now you could set them but not read them back.

`shpyrd sleep shop` and `shpyrd pg sleep db --project shop`, with no flag, now say which applies, where it comes from and the state now, and how to change it. The project's **Resources** page says the same on the web row and on each dedicated database, and a **Sleep** button sets it: the workspace's default, a quiet period with its waking mode, or never. `shpyrd pg info` and the resources list carry the same words.
