`shpyrd workspace` showed apps at `<app>.acme.shpyrd.cloud`, but on shpyrd cloud they answer at `acme-<app>.shpyrd.app`. The APPS line now shows the address apps really get, as the dashboard already did, and so does the message after `shpyrd workspace address`.
