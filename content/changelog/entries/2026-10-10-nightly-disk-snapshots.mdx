---
title: "Nightly snapshots of every disk"
description: "Block volumes get a snapshot every night, on top of a database's own backups."
date: "2026-10-10"
type: improvement
draft: false
---

Every block volume on the cloud profiles is snapshotted nightly. A database keeps its own backups as before; the snapshot is one more copy of the disk itself, for the day something goes wrong with a volume.
