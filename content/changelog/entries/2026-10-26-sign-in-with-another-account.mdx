---
title: "Sign in with another account"
description: "When a workspace refuses the account you signed in with, one click lets you choose another."
date: "2026-10-26"
type: feature
draft: false
---

If you have more than one shpyrd account and open a workspace with the wrong one, the sign-in page says why it was refused and offers **Sign in with another shpyrd account**: you sign out of shpyrd Accounts, sign in as the account you meant, and come back to the workspace. Before, signing in again brought back the same account, and a suspended workspace kept sending you around in a loop.

A workspace keeps its own session: to switch who is signed in to a workspace you are already in, sign out of it first.
