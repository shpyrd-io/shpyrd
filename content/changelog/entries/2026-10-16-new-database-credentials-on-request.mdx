---
title: "New credentials for a database on request"
description: "`shpyrd pg rotate` writes a new password and the apps bound to the database restart with it."
date: "2026-10-16"
type: feature
draft: false
---

`shpyrd pg rotate <name>` gives a database, shared or dedicated, a new password. It lands where the database's credentials live, and every app attached to the database restarts with it within a minute; nothing to copy by hand.
