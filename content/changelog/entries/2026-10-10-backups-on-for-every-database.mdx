---
title: "Backups on for every database"
description: "Continuous archiving and a daily base backup, with point-in-time recovery, for every PostgreSQL database."
date: "2026-10-10"
type: feature
draft: false
---

Every dedicated PostgreSQL database now has backups on by default: continuous archiving and a daily base backup, kept 14 days unless you choose otherwise. `shpyrd pg restore` brings any point in that window back into a new database, and `shpyrd pg backups` shows the base backups and the recovery window.
