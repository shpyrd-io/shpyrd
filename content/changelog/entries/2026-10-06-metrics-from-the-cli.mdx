You can inspect app processes, PostgreSQL databases, and Redis stores from the CLI. View a resource summary or export time series as JSON for charts, scripts, and agent-assisted analysis.

Use `shpyrd metrics`, `shpyrd pg metrics`, or `shpyrd redis metrics` to inspect the corresponding resource.
