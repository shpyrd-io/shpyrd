---

The workspace's General page shows the plan's ceilings beside what is in use: projects, instances, CPU, memory, block storage, object storage, buckets and databases. Anything that would pass a ceiling is refused before it is made, with the numbers ("This would bring the workspace to 150 GB of 100 GB."), and what already exists above a ceiling keeps working. A plan without disks says so in words and points at a bucket for files.
