---
title: "Billing stays reachable when a workspace is suspended"
description: "The owner of a suspended workspace with its own domain can sign in to billing and bring it back."
date: "2026-10-26"
type: fix
draft: false
---

A suspended workspace sends its owner to billing to bring it back. When the workspace's door was a domain of its own, the way there stopped at "Workspace suspended" and billing could not be reached. Signing in on the way to billing now works for those workspaces too; everything else in a suspended workspace stays closed.
