title: "Every instance wakes"
description: "An app with several instances under a sleep policy came back with one; it comes back with all of them."
date: "2026-10-22"
type: fix
draft: false
---

A process set to two or more instances and a sleep policy woke with a single instance after its first sleep, and stayed that way until the next deploy. A wake now restores the count the process declares.
