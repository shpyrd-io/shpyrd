---

The dashboard's dialogs say what the plan allows before you ask: whether disks are included, which kinds of database, how many instances, the cap of a bucket. A disk's growth reports where it stands (requested, pending while the provider grows it, completed, or failed with the reason), and a growth a storage class cannot do is refused at once. A size no machine of the cluster can hold is refused up front, naming the largest machine, instead of leaving a process waiting forever.
