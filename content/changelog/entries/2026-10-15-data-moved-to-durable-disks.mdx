---
title: "Existing data moved to durable disks"
description: "Every volume and database that lived on a machine's own disk now lives on provider block storage."
date: "2026-10-15"
type: improvement
draft: false
---

On shpyrd cloud, every volume and database that still kept its data on a machine's own disk was moved to provider block storage, one at a time, with a few minutes of pause each. Nothing changed on your side: addresses, credentials and mounts are the same, and the data now survives the loss of a machine.
