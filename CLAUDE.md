# shpyrd

Interface work is anything under `design/`, `content/` or `apps/`. Read
these before touching those folders:

- `design/DESIGN_FOR_AGENT.md`: how a design session starts, runs and ends.
  The person starts one with "enter design mode" ("entrar em modo design")
  and ends it with "finish design session" ("finalizar sessão de design").
- `apps/AGENTS.md`: how an application is made and what it must use.

The decisions behind them, and the order of the work, are in
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-29-design-content-apps.md`.
